**Subject:** Your Mentor Portal access — GLA Project Management System

---

Dear [Mentor Name],

You have been assigned as a faculty mentor for the [semester, e.g. Odd 2026]
capstone projects. Your teams and their work are managed through the Mentor
Portal.

**Getting in**

1. Go to https://mentor.glapms.in
2. Choose "Sign up" and register with your **@gla.ac.in** address. Please use
   the same address this email was sent to — the portal matches you against
   the mentor list we have already uploaded, and a different address will not
   be recognised.
3. After you submit, your registration waits for administrator approval. You
   will be able to log in once that is done.
4. If you forget your password later, use "Forgot password" on the login
   page. The reset link is valid for 15 minutes.

**What you will do there**

- **My Teams** — the teams assigned to you, their members, and their project.
- **Synopsis Submissions** — set what you want teams to submit, with a
  deadline, and review what comes back.
- **GitHub Analytics Hub** — an automated read of each team's repository:
  commit activity, code quality, and an overall score to support your
  assessment.
- **Evaluations** — submit your marks for each student, out of 30. This is
  done before the panel presentation. Please review carefully: once you
  confirm a submission, it is final and cannot be edited.
- **Presentations** — your scheduled sessions, once the coordinator sets them.

A short walkthrough runs the first time you open each page. You can replay it
at any time from the "?" icon in the top bar.

**If something looks wrong**

If a team is missing, an evaluation will not submit, or the portal does not
recognise your address, please reply to this email rather than creating a
second account.

Regards,
